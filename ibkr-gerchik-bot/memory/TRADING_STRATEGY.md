# Trading Strategy

## Core Rules

- Trade only deterministic Gerchik-style setups.
- Never send an order without a defined stop.
- Default to hold when macro or symbol news risk is elevated.
- Prefer liquid U.S. large-cap names with clean levels and manageable spread.
- Respect the daily loss guardrail and open-risk limits before any new trade.

## Buy-Side Gate

- Document the trade idea before the open.
- Re-check spread and fresh quotes at the open.
- Skip symbols with high-risk news, major earnings uncertainty, or duplicate exposure.
- Require reward/risk of at least 2:1.

## Intraday Rules

- Cut losers at or below -7%.
- Tighten stops on strong winners.
- Exit if the thesis breaks, even before stop loss is reached.

## Review Process

- Record every research pass, trade, adjustment, and end-of-day summary.
- Use the weekly review to identify what worked and what needs adjustment.
